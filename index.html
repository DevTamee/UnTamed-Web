Only the strongest win.

UnTamed is a Minecraft server built on clan wars, an open PvP arena, crates and frequent events.

What you'll find here

Four things we care about.

Clan wars

Form a clan, rally your people and settle it in war. The strongest clan earns the name.

The arena

Type /arena and you're in the fight. No waiting, just PvP.

Crates

Open crates with vote keys, rank keys and keys won in events.

Events

Frequent server events with prizes. Follow our socials and Discord to catch the next one.

Ranks

Everyone starts as Stray. Tap a rank to see what it includes.

Prices are in Philippine pesos (PHP). To get a rank, join our Discord and open a ticket. Staff will handle payment and set your rank in-game.
